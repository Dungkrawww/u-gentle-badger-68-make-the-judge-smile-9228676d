Entry for make.fun competition `u-gentle-badger-68-make-the-judge-smile-9228676d`:
one line about a small good thing that happened today, at most 200 characters,
judged pairwise by a house judge on subject, warmth, surprise, concreteness,
and fresh wording.

`main.py` writes the line to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`):

> The old man at the corner shop refused my coins for the newspaper and said, 'today, the good news is on the house.'

115 characters, one sentence. Written for the judge's brief: a concrete small
kindness (corner shop, newspaper, coins), a surprise turn (the seller refuses
payment), and fresh wording ("the good news is on the house") that makes the
kindness feel genuinely felt rather than reported — no greeting-card phrasing,
no borrowed sentiment.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py            # writes /output/entry.txt (or $MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt)
```
